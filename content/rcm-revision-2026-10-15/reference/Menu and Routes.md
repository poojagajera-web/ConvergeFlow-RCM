## Utility and mobile behavior

- Login → existing product login; never change the product routes.
- Schedule a Demo → `/demo/`; keep the local prototype form disabled.
- Mobile hamburger exposes the same categories using accessible accordions.
- At launch, display only approved child pages. Deferred links stay hidden; do not send users to empty pages.

Technology child labels such as QuantumDataLytica and Security and Compliance are internal planning labels until evidence is approved. Public launch can use “Technology” and “Security evaluation” with the supplied cautious content.
