## About ConvergeFlow and the work your team reviews

The product discussion starts with the tasks your staff needs to understand. An upcoming appointment provides context for insurance review. A verification status or eligibility flag points to a question that needs attention. Claims, balances and reports extend that review into the later stages of the cycle.

ConvergeFlow includes interfaces for those areas. The details that matter to your practice include what the information means, where it comes from and who takes the next action. Confirm those details during evaluation.

## Product questions

### Does ConvergeFlow cover every stage automatically?
Confirm the available capabilities for each stage during evaluation. The revenue-cycle structure explains how the work connects, while a screen or menu shows an interface. Neither establishes automatic execution, payer coverage, processing accuracy or a specific outcome for your practice without further confirmation and evidence.

### Is the product only relevant to dental practices?
Dental and medical practices have separate evaluation paths on this site. The observed account was dental-focused, so medical-specific workflows and requirements need confirmation. Bring your specialty, payer and system questions to the discussion rather than treating the audience label as proof that every use case is supported.
