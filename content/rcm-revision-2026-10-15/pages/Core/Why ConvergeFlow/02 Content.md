## Why ConvergeFlow: start with the task and its context

Appointment details sit beside verification work in the product's queue. Practice, date and status filters provide ways to narrow the view. In a demo, examine whether the context answers the questions your staff needs to resolve before the visit.

Ask how a status is established and what information is available when an item remains unresolved. Seeing a status is useful for evaluation; the next question is whether its meaning and source fit your process.

---

## Inspect the reasons an item needs attention

The eligibility view presents risk flags alongside upcoming appointments. Review the labels, the supporting information and the follow-up your team would need. Confirm how flags are generated and what they mean for your workflow before treating them as a prediction or decision.

---

## Look beyond the first review step

Claims, receivables, reporting and audit views broaden the discussion beyond appointment preparation. Explore how your team would inspect outstanding work across the cycle. Ask which fields, filters and activities are available for the scope you need.

The [services overview](/services/) organizes the broader lifecycle. Use it to identify additional requirements, including stages that need separate capability confirmation.

## Judge fit with a concrete checklist

| What to examine | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| Appointment and verification queue | Which context and statuses your staff can inspect |
| Eligibility flags | Their meaning, information source and review responsibility |
| Claims and receivables views | Available fields, balances and follow-up scope |
| Reports and activity records | Included information, reporting boundaries and access |
| Your practice systems | Supported connections, responsibilities and implementation scope |

Keep the answers tied to the appointment, claim or balance your team needs to understand. Record what the product shows and which questions need follow-up.

## Evaluation questions

### What should we compare in a product demo?
Use the same appointment, eligibility and receivables scenario across the products you are considering. Compare the context shown, actions demonstrated, responsibilities and questions still open. Keep the scope consistent so your team can judge which questions each product answers and which details need more evidence.

### What would make the demo more useful?
Choose one de-identified workflow and list the information your team needs at each handoff. Ask to review the relevant views and note what remains unconfirmed. This creates a concrete basis for deciding what further evidence, integration detail or commercial clarification you need before an implementation decision.
