## What to review in your ConvergeFlow demo

Start with the part of the cycle that needs a clearer handoff. You can ask about appointment-based verification, eligibility review, claims views, receivables and reporting. Confirm available capabilities, integrations and review responsibilities for your requirements.

Bring your practice type, relevant PMS or EHR and the questions that matter to your team. For medical or specialty workflows, describe the requirements explicitly rather than assume that a general product view establishes fit.

## Questions before requesting a demo

### Does submitting the form book an appointment?
Submitting this form sends a demo request when the request is successfully received. It does not confirm an appointment time. Use the discussion to review your requirements and the available product views; do not assume a specific duration, response time or implementation date from the request.

### Should I upload a patient example?
Do not send patient records or clinical documents through this form. Describe your workflow in general terms and leave out names, identifiers and insurance details tied to a person. A de-identified scenario can help frame the questions without placing sensitive information in a marketing inquiry.
