## Prepare for a ConvergeFlow pricing discussion

A clear requirements summary makes commercial questions more specific. Describe the parts of the cycle you want to review, the systems involved and the people who need to use the information. Do not include patient records or identifiable billing documents.

| Bring this context | Ask this question |
|---|---|
| Workflow priorities | Which capabilities are included in the proposal? |
| Practice structure and locations | What organizational scope does the proposal cover? |
| PMS, EHR and other systems | Which connections are supported, and what work is required? |
| Review and reporting needs | What access and reporting are included? |
| Expected rollout responsibilities | Which setup, training or ongoing services are included or separate? |

These are questions to resolve, not a published pricing formula. Confirm any proposed fees and conditions in the commercial agreement.

---

## Review the total proposal

Compare the included scope with the work your team expects to do. Ask about recurring charges, any setup or usage charges, optional services and the conditions that could change the cost. Keep the answers with the proposal so the comparison remains clear.

Also review the term, renewal, cancellation and support provisions. An estimate or calculator can help organize assumptions, but it cannot establish a contractual price or guarantee a return.

## Questions about cost and terms

### Where can I find a published price list?
This page does not list fixed prices or packages. Use the demo request to start a discussion about your requirements and the relevant commercial proposal. Ask which charges are included, which may be separate and what scope or conditions apply before making a purchasing decision.

### Is a free assessment or a trial included?
Confirm any trial, assessment or promotional offer directly during the commercial discussion. No such offer is promised on this page. Review any eligibility conditions, duration and included work in writing, along with the terms for continuing or ending the arrangement after an offer.
