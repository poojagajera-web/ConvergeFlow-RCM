## Contact ConvergeFlow with the context that matters

A short description of your workflow gives the product conversation a useful starting point. Include your practice type and the stage you need to review, such as appointment preparation, eligibility, claims or receivables. You can mention a PMS or EHR name without sharing account credentials.

For group practices, explain which roles need to review the work and whether multiple locations are involved. For a medical or specialty practice, describe the specific requirements you need to confirm.

---

## Keep sensitive information out of your inquiry

Use business contact details and general workflow descriptions. Do not send patient names, member identifiers, insurance documents, clinical information, passwords or screenshots containing sensitive records. Explain the question without attaching the underlying patient or account data.

## Contact questions

### Can I use the demo request for patient or clinical questions?
Use the demo request for business product evaluation. Do not include personal medical questions, patient records or clinical documents. Frame your question around the workflow and the information your staff needs to review, without sharing information that identifies a patient or provides access to an account.

### What should I send before discussing an integration?
Start with the system name and the general information flow you need to support. Do not share login credentials, API secrets or exported patient data. Ask which interfaces and transactions are available, then confirm the required access and responsibilities through an approved process before connecting systems.
