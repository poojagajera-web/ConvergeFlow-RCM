## How ConvergeFlow works: start with the handoffs

A useful evaluation follows the information your team needs at each handoff. Identify what starts the work, who reviews it and what the next person needs to see. This keeps the discussion connected to your practice rather than a list of screens.

### Map the practice systems

List where appointments, insurance details and billing records are held. Bring the names of your PMS or EHR and any other systems involved. Ask which connections are available and what information would need to move between them.

### Define the payer questions

Describe the coverage, benefits or authorization questions your team needs to resolve. Separate the information you need from the decisions your staff must make. Confirm the scope for your payers and procedures during evaluation.

### Review the product views

Look at appointment context, verification status and eligibility flags together. Then review the claims, receivables and reporting views relevant to your priorities. Ask what a status means, where its information comes from and how an unresolved item is represented.

### Agree who reviews exceptions

Assign a person or role to each unresolved question in your proposed process. Discuss how that person would find the context and identify the next action. Confirm any routing, notifications or escalation requirements instead of assuming they are included.

### Choose what to measure

Define the workload, review period and baseline you would use to assess a change. Keep measures specific to the workflow you are evaluating. Product views and general workflow goals do not establish an improvement without a consistent comparison.

---

## Bring a scenario, without patient information

A de-identified example makes the evaluation easier to follow. Describe an upcoming appointment with an unresolved insurance question, a claim needing review or an outstanding balance. Leave out names, identifiers, documents and clinical details.

---

## Questions about the evaluation

### Will the demo confirm every integration?
Use the demo to identify the connections your practice needs and ask what is supported. A system name on a screen does not establish a working integration. Confirm transactions, fields, access requirements and responsibilities before making an implementation decision or changing any existing systems.

### Is this an implementation schedule?
This page describes an evaluation path. Implementation scope, responsibilities and timing need agreement after your requirements and available capabilities have been reviewed. Do not treat the steps as a promise of automated execution, a specific go-live date or a commitment to replace your current process.
