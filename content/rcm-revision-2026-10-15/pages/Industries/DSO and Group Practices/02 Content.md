## What does DSO revenue cycle management need to show?

Start with the distinction between practice-level work and group-level oversight. List the appointments, claims and balances local teams review, then identify the questions your central team needs answered. Bring that structure to the demo. A practice selector provides context, but it does not establish consolidated reporting, access separation or support for your group’s operating model.

---

## How should work move between local and central teams?

Define the next owner at each handoff before evaluating automation. Choose an unresolved eligibility question, a claim requiring review and an outstanding balance. Ask how the available product represents each item and what information the receiving team can inspect. Confirm whether assignment, escalation and shared work queues meet your requirements rather than assuming those capabilities.

---

## How can you evaluate reporting across practices?

Compare report scope before comparing totals. Ask which practice and date filters apply, whether records can be combined and how access affects what a reviewer sees. ConvergeFlow has report controls and an audit activity view; group-wide aggregation and permissions remain requirements to confirm. Request evidence for the reporting structure your organization needs.

---

## What should your group prepare before the demo?

Prepare a list of practice systems, locations and review roles. Describe where responsibility changes between offices and the central team. Include the outputs needed for practice meetings and group reviews. Use synthetic records to discuss the workflow, and keep any rollout sequence conditional on confirmed integrations, access controls and implementation requirements.

## Questions about DSO and group practices

### Does a practice selector confirm support for our entire group?

No. A practice selector demonstrates practice context, not your complete group workflow. Confirm the supported organization structure, access boundaries, shared responsibilities and reporting scope in a ConvergeFlow evaluation. Ask how the available features handle differences between practices before deciding how a rollout would work for your organization.
