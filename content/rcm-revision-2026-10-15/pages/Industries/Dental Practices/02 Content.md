## Where should dental revenue cycle management begin?

Begin with the upcoming appointment and the information needed for review. The dental-focused account reviewed in ConvergeFlow showed appointment date, provider, procedure, insurance carrier and verification status together. The screen includes search and practice, date and status filters. Ask how they narrow the schedule in your workflow. Ask how those details reach the queue and which information still needs confirmation.

---

## What should your team review before the visit?

Review the displayed eligibility flags alongside the appointment context. A flag gives staff a reason to examine an item; it does not establish a coverage or claim outcome. Discuss how your team records unresolved questions, who follows them up and when the next person should review the record. Confirm available payer responses and benefit fields in the demo.

---

## How does the discussion continue after the visit?

Continue with the claims, submission queue, ERA/EOB, patient statement and receivables views. These areas were visible in the reviewed product, but processing was not tested. Bring a billing handoff your staff needs to understand and ask how records, statuses and responsibilities relate across the available modules.
