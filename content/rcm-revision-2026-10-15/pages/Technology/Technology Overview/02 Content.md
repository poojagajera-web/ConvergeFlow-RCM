## What should revenue cycle management technology connect?

Start by identifying the source of appointments, insurance information, claims and balances. Ask which systems and versions are supported, what information is read or written and how changes are handled. A displayed PMS name or connection field is a reason to ask for detail; it is not evidence that your practice has a working integration.

---

## How should you examine workflow behavior?

Follow a synthetic example through the available views and distinguish displayed information from executed actions. Ask what starts a task, what staff must review and what happens when information is missing. Discuss scheduling, routing and retries as requirements to verify. Do not assume that a visible control establishes background automation or a completed transaction.

---

## What can reports and activity views help you evaluate?

Use the report and audit interfaces to define the information your reviewers need. The observed reports area includes receivables, patient and appointment export controls. The audit view includes timestamp, user, action, resource, details and IP address columns. Confirm the output, event coverage, access and retention requirements; exports and audit completeness were not tested.

---

## How do you turn a demo into technical requirements?

Record each requirement, the behavior demonstrated, the evidence supplied and the question still open. Include systems, payer information, staff responsibilities and reporting scope. Bring access and data-handling questions to the security discussion. Agree what still needs validation before planning connections, operating changes or deployment.

## Questions about technology overview

### Does a feature label establish a working integration or automation?

No. A label identifies an area to examine, not a validated connection or completed action. Use a ConvergeFlow technical evaluation to confirm supported systems, data flows, triggers and exception behavior. Record what is demonstrated and ask for the evidence your team needs before planning an implementation or relying on an automated step.
