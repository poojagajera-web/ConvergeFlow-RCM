## Where should an RCM security evaluation begin?

Begin with the proposed information flow and the systems involved. Identify which records your workflow would use, where information would move and who would be responsible for each step. Ask the product team to describe the available implementation and provide the documentation your organization requires. Keep unanswered questions open until the responsible reviewer has assessed the evidence.

---

## What should you ask about access and credentials?

Ask how users receive access, how permissions are changed and how access is removed. If a workflow needs external-system credentials, request details about storage, handling, use and administrative access. Confirm the controls for your proposed setup instead of inferring them from interface labels. This draft does not establish a particular access model or credential-management implementation.

---

## What does an audit activity screen establish?

An audit activity screen establishes visible fields to inspect, not complete event coverage or a compliance determination. The observed ConvergeFlow audit view includes timestamp, user, action, resource, details and IP address columns. Ask which events are recorded, who can view or export them, how long records remain available and how your reviewers would obtain supporting evidence.

---

## What evidence should your team request next?

Request the documentation relevant to your data-handling, access, retention and incident-response requirements. Ask which agreements apply to the proposed relationship and who can answer follow-up questions. The appropriate review depends on your organization’s responsibilities and intended use. Use synthetic examples during early discussions and keep sensitive records out of the public demo request.

## Questions about security evaluation

### Does an audit trail establish a security certification?

No. An audit interface does not establish a certification, regulatory compliance or complete control coverage. ConvergeFlow’s observed audit fields provide a starting point for questions about activity visibility. Request the evidence your organization needs and have the responsible reviewers assess it before connecting systems or making an implementation decision.
