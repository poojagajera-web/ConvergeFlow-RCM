## Use RCM resources to frame the next question

Start with the stage that creates uncertainty for your team. The questions below help you describe a workflow without sending patient records or assuming a particular product feature will resolve it.

### Before the visit

Identify which appointment and insurance details staff need to review. Separate eligibility questions from the benefits detail or authorization information that may need further attention. Note who owns an unresolved item and when your team would look at it again.

### During billing review

Describe the documentation, charge information and claim status that your team needs to understand. Keep the question specific: which information is missing, who can review it and what is required before the next step?

### During payment and receivables follow-up

Connect the balance question to its account context and review period. Define which aging categories, payment information or outstanding work your team needs to inspect. Use consistent dates and definitions when comparing reports.

## Resource questions

### Do the guides establish what ConvergeFlow can automate?
General guides explain workflow concepts and questions. They do not establish that ConvergeFlow supports every transaction, payer or stage described. Use the relevant product discussion to confirm available capabilities, data flows and responsibilities for your practice before relying on a guide as an implementation plan.

### Can I share a workflow example in a demo request?
You can describe the workflow in general terms without patient information. Leave out names, identifiers, documents and clinical details. Explain the question your staff needs to answer and the next action they need to take, then use the discussion to identify the relevant product views and remaining requirements.
